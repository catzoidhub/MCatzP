# Support

MCatzP is made by one person in Indonesia — Mahryan Rakhmatullah (Ryan N.R.) — with two others helping, and is free and open source. This page says honestly what
you can expect.

## Where to ask

| You want to … | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Report a bug | **Report a bug** in any cat's Settings, or [open an issue](https://github.com/catzoidhub/MCatzP/issues/new) |
| Report a security problem | **Not in a public issue** — see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) |
| Read the docs first | [catzoid.com/docs/mcatzp](https://catzoid.com/docs/mcatzp), with [Troubleshooting](https://catzoid.com/docs/troubleshooting#mcatzp) |
| Talk with other users | [catzoid.com/community](https://catzoid.com/community) |

Asking your assistant to call `mcatzp_status` first often answers the question: it says which cats are reachable and what
to do about the others. Please add what it says to your report.

## What to expect

- **Issues and bug reports** are read and answered as time allows — there is no fixed response time. Security
  reports come first, then everything else.
- **Pull requests** are welcome — small and focused is best. Open an issue first for anything big, so nobody builds
  something that will not be merged. The first time, the CLA check asks you to sign the
  [contributor licence agreement](CLA.md); you keep the copyright of your work.

## Free, and staying free

No paid support and no Pro tier — everyone gets the same help. If MCatzP saves you time and you want to help keep it going,
see [catzoid.com/community](https://catzoid.com/community).
